FitBase — your gym, your plan, a coach only when you want one.

FitBase is a fitness training app. It turns whatever equipment you have — a rack in the garage, a few dumbbells, a building gym — into a real weekly training plan, and adjusts that plan every time you log a workout. Browse a library of 1,324 exercises with animated demonstrations, build workouts, track your training history, and get AI-generated coaching based on your goals and the equipment you actually own. When you want human eyes on your form, hire a real coach at their rate through the built-in marketplace — no standing appointment required.

Training on FitBase is free. Sign in with your email or your Google account. Coaching services are optional and paid; payments are processed by Stripe.
